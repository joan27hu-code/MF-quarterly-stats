# Topic

Quarterly multifamily market reports from large institutional brokerage firms
(JLL, CBRE, Cushman & Wakefield, Berkadia and Colliers), consolidated into one set of
market stats for my Northern California markets:

- San Jose
- Palo Alto
- Mountain View
- San Francisco
- Oakland
- Pleasanton
- Roseville

Firms don't all report at the same level. Some report a whole metro, such as
San Jose / Silicon Valley, the East Bay or Sacramento. Others report submarkets,
such as Palo Alto, Mountain View, Tri-Valley/Pleasanton or Roseville/Placer
County. Each claim's subject should name the firm, the area exactly as the
report labels it, and the metric. For example: "CBRE, San Jose metro, overall
vacancy rate".

# Questions

1. Vacancy: what vacancy rate does each firm report for each of my markets each
   quarter? Does the report call it total, stabilized, or occupancy (100 minus
   vacancy)?
2. Asking rent: what average market asking rent does each firm report for each
   market each quarter? Is it per unit per month or per square foot?
3. Effective rent: what average effective rent (after concessions) does each
   firm report for each market each quarter? Where reported, how large are the
   concessions?
4. Rent growth: how much did asking and effective rents change from the prior
   quarter and from the same quarter last year, by market and firm?
5. Absorption: how many units of net absorption did each market record in the
   quarter? Is the figure for the quarter alone, year to date, or trailing
   12 months?
6. Supply: how many units were delivered in the quarter, and how many are under
   construction, by market?
7. Comparison: where two or more firms report the same market and quarter, how
   far apart are their figures? Which differences come from definitions (area
   boundaries, stabilized vs. total, asking vs. effective) rather than from the
   market?
